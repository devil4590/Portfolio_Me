About me

A student engineer and a poet, working on the same ground.

I'm a final-year Computer Engineering student at UET Taxila, Pakistan. I'm drawn to software engineering, web development, machine learning and data analytics, mostly the practical kind, where a working application or a clear chart makes something easier for someone.

I'm also a Pakistani poet and literary writer from Kallar Kahar, in the Salt Range. My writing follows landscapes, memory, family and tradition, and asks what happens to them as technology changes the way we live.

I work across both, which is why I think of myself as connecting the hillside and the hard drive.

The hillside

Salt Range ridges, lakes, family stories, traditions, and the slow work of noticing.

The hard drive

Web apps, datasets, models and workflows built to be useful and clear.

Same curiosity, two languages.

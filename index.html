About

The hillside and the hard drive

I write, and I build. Some days that means debugging a React component until midnight; other days it means sitting with a half-finished poem about the hills near my hometown. I don't experience these as separate lives — they're two ways of paying attention to the same world.

I'm a final-year Computer Engineering student at the University of Engineering and Technology (UET), Taxila, Pakistan. My technical interests sit across software engineering, web development, machine learning, and data analytics — I like taking a problem from a rough idea to something that actually works and that people can use.

Alongside that, I'm a Pakistani writer from Kallar Kahar. My writing explores landscapes, memory, family, tradition, and the quiet, sometimes uneasy relationship between where I'm from and the technology I now spend my days building.

"I keep finding myself writing about the same hills I once tried to leave, in the same week I'm debugging a model meant to predict something far away from them."

I don't see a contradiction in being both a developer and a writer — code and language are both ways of shaping something unclear into something legible. This site is where both halves of that live side by side.
